Note by Yang for the OC3 Hywind Spar model

The dynamics of the spar model are signficantly influenced by the platform acceleration as the CM is far below the MSL. 
However, the platform accelerations are transferred from AQWA based on a backward difference of velocity. The inevitably numerical errors may result in instability of the simulation.
Therefore, we need to set a moderate initial condition in mitigate the transient effect during the start-up stage.

In the example, I have set a zero initial rotor speed, and 5 degs of the blade pitch, which allows the inflow wind (11.4m/s turbulent wind) to produce sufficient torque to moderately start the turbine.
